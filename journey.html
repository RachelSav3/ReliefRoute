About map data and examples

Live toilets come from community-edited OpenStreetMap and are not independently verified. Opening hours and accessibility may be unknown. If the data service is unavailable, eight fictional examples are shown along a suggested straight-line corridor and clearly labelled. These examples are not actual toilets and have no directions. Routes are indicative and require a connection to external services.
