The problem

Not knowing can change everything.

For people living with Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis, MS and other conditions involving urgent toilet needs, a day out or unfamiliar journey can involve extra planning. Knowing a toilet exists isn't enough: you need to know whether you can actually use it.

1

Is there a toilet?

Find facilities near unfamiliar places, including sports grounds and community venues.

2

Can I use it?

Understand public access, purchase rules, opening times and accessibility.

3

What if I'm travelling?

See potential stops before setting off, with clear limits on what is verified.

The inspiration

Relief Route was inspired by the experience of attending a child's sports activity at a football pitch without knowing where a publicly accessible toilet was. The idea is to make this information easier to find, before uncertainty becomes an emergency.

This describes the project's motivation; it is not a medical claim or a user testimonial.

The people and the plan

Designed for a specific need.

Who uses it?

Primary users: people of all ages in Ireland living with IBD who need dependable toilet access information when travelling or visiting unfamiliar places.

Who supports it?

Potential funders include accessibility grant programmes, local authorities and charitable partners. No partnership or funding is currently confirmed.

How could it be sustained?

A proposed pilot could be supported by grant funding and voluntary sponsorship, keeping essential toilet search free. A real rollout would require costs for hosting, location services, verification, accessibility testing and moderation.

Illustrative model, not secured funding or a financial forecast.

Requirements and scope

What the technology must do.

Working in this prototype

  • Search 10 clearly labelled fictional facilities
  • Filter by access rules and step-free availability
  • Plan a journey using a predefined sample corridor
  • Submit and display ratings and access feedback
  • Save pending business registrations locally
  • Retain demo changes after refresh using local browser storage

Planned for a real service

  • GPS-based nearest toilet search and live mapping
  • Real driving routes and stop-gap warnings
  • Verified business identities and access checks
  • Secure review moderation and fraud protection
  • Accessible directions and offline route downloads
  • Privacy and security review before deployment
Research and validation

Existing services and testing.

Relief Route would need to offer a meaningful improvement over existing toilet-location services. Two relevant starting points for comparison are Pee.ie (Irish public toilet mapping) and the We Can't Wait initiative (toilet access for people with IBD). Review their current features at source before submission and add your actual checked date here.

Research in progress: Two dated comparisons will be added following testing. Ask at least one person who did not build the prototype to test it. Record what they tried, what confused or broke, and the specific change you made. Do not claim testing you haven't completed.

Suggested independent test tasks

  1. Find a sample toilet in Drogheda that needs no purchase.
  2. Plan a sample Drogheda–Athlone journey.
  3. Submit a review, refresh the page, and check that it remains.
  4. Register a sample business, refresh, and check its pending status.

Independent user-testing findings will be published after testing and revisions.
