The story behind reliefroute.

My Why.

Because sometimes, the smallest things can make the biggest difference.

I was diagnosed with Crohn's disease at 23, and since then I've learned that living with a chronic condition can shape even the simplest parts of everyday life.

Something as ordinary as going for a walk, attending an event, travelling somewhere new or spending a day out can come with an extra layer of planning. Where is the nearest toilet? Will I be able to access it? What happens if I need one urgently?

For many people, these are questions they rarely have to think about. For those living with Crohn's, colitis and other conditions involving urgency, they can influence where we go and how confident we feel.

I created Relief Route because I believe everyone deserves the freedom to enjoy everyday life without the constant worry of finding a toilet.

It's about independence, dignity and confidence.

Why we're building it

Because everyone deserves the freedom to go.

For people living with Crohn's, colitis, MS and other conditions that can make toilet access urgent, everyday outings can take extra planning. Relief Route is a prototype concept for a more inclusive way to navigate the world.

Good to know

Built with accessibility in mind.

Can I find a live toilet location right now?

This is an early concept prototype. The finder now queries OpenStreetMap for mapped toilets. Data may be incomplete or outdated and is not verified; a labelled sample demonstration is also available.

Can I plan a real journey?

The route-planning interface is a preview. Real routing and accessible facility stops are planned for future development.

How will reviews work?

The proposed platform will allow people to share practical information about cleanliness, access and facilities, with safeguards for useful, respectful feedback.
