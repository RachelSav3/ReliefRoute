My Why

A simple need. A big impact.

Living with Crohn’s disease has shown me how much uncertainty about toilet access can affect everyday plans. Even a short journey or a day out can mean thinking ahead about where a toilet might be — and whether it will actually be accessible when needed.

Relief Route grew from the idea that finding this information should be simpler, clearer and less stressful for anyone who needs it.

“I wanted to make everyday journeys a little easier for people who understand that uncertainty.”

This project begins with people living with IBD, of all ages, and has the potential to support many others who need dependable toilet information.

Simple. Practical. Made for real life.

About the prototype and its data

Relief Route is a student prototype, not a guaranteed toilet-access service. Live mapped facilities come from OpenStreetMap contributors and may be incomplete or outdated. Fictional demonstration locations are labelled as samples and must not be used for navigation. User-added toilets and demo feedback are stored in the current browser only, not shared publicly. The photographs on this page are illustrative AI-generated images, not photographs of actual listed facilities.
